Courses Offered

KBTCOE offers the following undergraduate and postgraduate programs:

Mechanical Engineering

4-Year UG Program focused on design, thermal, and production engineering.

Computer Engineering

4-Year UG Program covering programming, algorithms, and software systems.

Information Technology

4-Year UG Program in networking, cloud computing, and data-driven systems.

Electronics & Telecommunication

4-Year UG Program in communication systems, embedded design, and IoT.

Civil Engineering

4-Year UG Program in structural design, construction, and surveying.

Instrumentation Engineering

4-Year UG Program in control systems, sensors, and automation.

MBA

2-Year PG Program in management, marketing, finance, and HR.
